# Self-graveyard triggers, persistent owners and spell-only protection

The new worker packet was independently checked against complete local Oracle text and the current producer. It identifies ten cards: Dread, Emrakul, the Aeons Torn, Guile, Hostility, Kozilek, Butcher of Truth, Purity, Serra Avatar, Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre, Vigor and Worldspine Wurm. Three narrow corrections preserve every other operand.

The fork's from-anywhere card-event adapter appended `Not(Token)` even for the exact `SingleCard(ThisCard)` self-reference. A named self-reference denotes the source object, including a token copy; applicable triggers occur before that token ceases to exist. The adapter now omits that extra predicate only for this self-reference. Ordinary card descriptions and explicitly named-card filters retain their nontoken domain. The matched source, owner domain, destination and graveyard functioning zone are unchanged. This follows CR 201.5 and 111.7 in the [pinned primary rules](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt); no game-state execution is claimed.

Emrakul, Kozilek and Ulamog move their owner's whole graveyard and then shuffle. The owner binding previously queried the matched card through its current graveyard membership, so the second instruction could lose its player after the first moved that card. The exact event-card owner query now uses the captured event owner, matching the existing event-player owner binding. The English owner instructions remain unchanged. This does not rewrite references used to move a card: all seven atomic self-shuffle instructions retain their destination object, current graveyard guard and `OwnersLibraryShuffled` operation. Owner queries without a matching event are refused.

Emrakul's printed colored-spell protection was emitted as protection from colored objects, incorrectly covering colored permanent sources and blockers. Its English quality now emits the existing `FromSpells(IsColored)` form. The syntax validator's two hardcoded `FromSpells` examples are generalized to its already-declared `SPELLS` parameter, matching the frozen Rust `FromSpells(Box<Spells>)` type. The color predicate stays identical; only the protected source kind becomes Spell. Plain colored protection remains Any. This source correction replies to existing [upstream issue 129](https://github.com/i5jb/mtgish/issues/129); the self-trigger and owner corrections are fork binding defects, so no misleading English-parser issue is filed for them.
