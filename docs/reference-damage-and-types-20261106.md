# Independent damage parts, target toughness and subtype replacement

Complete local Oracle text and actual grammar causes confirm five pending catalog findings and one additional caller, Reptil, Dinomorpher. Seven English templates are corrected using existing typed source constructors. No Rust lowering, schema or backend contracts change.

Bellowing Fiend's printed damage is one simultaneous instruction with two parts. The template emitted two sequential damage operations. Burning Sun's Avatar and Punish the Enemy instead merged their two target recipient sets into a union, losing a second damage part if one creature-planeswalker occupied both target slots. Each now emits the existing `PermanentDealsMultipleDamage` or `SpellDealsMultipleDamage` constructor with two explicit `DamageToRecipients` entries. Sources, announced targets, optional/required cardinalities, actual damaged-creature controller, prevention and real-card amounts remain identical. All three templates also retain their independently captured second amount; synthetic unequal-amount controls prove the second capture is no longer discarded. No recipient equality alias or change to union semantics is involved.

Garruk, Apex Predator independently gains life equal to its targeted creature's toughness after attempting destruction. Its English template read only the successful destruction receipt, incorrectly producing zero when an otherwise legal target is indestructible. It now uses `ToughnessOfPermanent(Ref_TargetPermanent)`, which lowers to the target's CurrentOrLastKnown toughness. The loyalty cost, destroy operation, independent gain-life instruction and all other loyalty abilities are preserved. Make Yourself Useful explicitly conditions life gain on successful destruction; its parser output and corresponding conditional/receipt control remain unchanged.

Nogi, Draco-Zealot becomes a Dragon without wording that retains its prior creature types. Its template now sets the Dragon type rather than adding it. Full caller review finds the same error in both of Reptil's Dinosaur Hero forms: one existing `SetCreatureTypes([Dinosaur, Hero])` replaces both additions, retaining the two printed types together. Costs, power/toughness, keyword grants, durations, Nogi's trigger/condition and other abilities remain identical. Explicit AddCreatureType operands and wording that retains other types are unchanged; this is a correction to those exact English templates. The established distinction follows CR 205.1a in the [pinned primary rules](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt).

Seven normalized-IR tests include six actual-card regressions that fail before/pass after and an unchanged explicit-addition/conditional-destruction control. Parser regressions cover all six cards, three unequal second amounts, and Make Yourself Useful. Complete source and IR comparisons prove exactly six records change in seven reviewed program parts; every other complete operand and all other records are identical. Full-corpus parsing identifies every actual caller of the changed templates. The checked-in source formats and relevant fixtures are regenerated from that parser output.

Read-only source-name and face-name comparison finds zero overlap with the 601 pending publication cards at `4a007d87`. Public schema stays 178; no worker files, gold, aliases, pins, engine contracts or publication artifacts change. The catalog now has 105 entries: 66 fixed and 39 pending, with eight unresolved contract questions. The 27 conservative erased-domain sites remain pending and unchanged.

Detailed receipts are in `/mnt/vizier/reference-damage-and-types-20261106`; small durable receipts are copied into the integration evidence's `damage-and-types` directory after final validation.
