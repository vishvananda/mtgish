# Printed spell casters, attachment hosts and self-damage parts

Independent review of complete local Oracle text confirms four pending findings and three additional affinity callers. Five English templates change using existing typed source forms. The patch changes no Rust lowering, public schema, backend contract or worker file.

The three `PotentialSpells you cast have affinity` templates discarded the caster qualifier. They now retain the captured spell-type predicate and add `CastByPlayer(You)`, as the adjacent storm/cascade/convoke templates already do. The existing cast-provenance filter reads recorded caster information. This fixes Sami, Wildcat Captain; Tezzeret, Master of the Bridge; Mycosynth Golem; Pearl-Ear, Imperial Advisor; and Witherbloom, the Balancer. Each existing artifact-creature, creature-or-planeswalker, enchantment or instant-or-sorcery filter is preserved, along with its affinity quality and every other ability. Tezzeret's existing +2 CreateValueX binding remains unchanged.

All seven local Oracle cards with you-cast affinity wording were inspected. The Grand Goatnapper already has its correct player-scoped grant and remains unchanged. Memnarchitect is on the pre-existing ignore list with a distinct unsupported affinity quality; this patch neither claims its coverage nor changes that list. The five actually parsed callers of the three corrected templates are included in the preservation proof. Explicit unrestricted StackSpellsEffect source remains unrestricted.

Simic Guildmage's Aura relocation now reads `ControllerOfPermanent(HostPermanentOf(Ref_TargetPermanent))`. The old expression read the Aura target's controller. The new host is still chosen during resolution, excludes the Aura's current host, and remains a permanent with the current host's controller. The targeted Aura, attachment requirement, choice count, actual selected new host, mana cost and separate counter-transfer ability remain identical.

Sunflare Shaman's any-target and self damage are two explicit simultaneous parts. Its prior recipient union lost a damage part when the Shaman itself occupied the any-target slot. The template now uses the existing PermanentDealsMultipleDamage constructor, preserving the source, any-target announcement, mana/tap cost, preventability and both identical Elemental-card graveyard counts. No extra Creature predicate is added to the printed Elemental-card domain. Self-Destruct explicitly excludes its damaging creature from the other target; its disjoint recipient union is source-correct and remains unchanged as a negative control.

Seven actual-card IR regressions fail before/pass after. Two negative controls pass before and after for unrestricted affinity source and Self-Destruct. Parser regressions cover all seven cards and the unchanged disjoint case. Fresh complete parsing changes exactly seven source records. Full typed export proves exactly seven corresponding IR program parts change; every other complete operand and all other records are identical. The checked-in source formats and current fixtures are regenerated. All 182 audit findings and full usages are identical; only input hashes are refreshed. No new literal review or semantic exception is added, and existing review history remains intact.

The seven source names and face names have zero overlap with the 601 publication cards at `4a007d87`. The public schema stays 178. No reference gold, equality aliases, prepared AST pins, engine contracts or publication artifacts change. The final catalog has 108 entries: 73 fixed and 35 pending, plus ten normalization questions and eight unresolved contract questions. Twenty-seven erased-domain filter sites in 27 records remain conservatively withheld. Zask's alleged missing Creature predicate remains unestablished and unchanged, with its separate moving-incarnation question still pending.
