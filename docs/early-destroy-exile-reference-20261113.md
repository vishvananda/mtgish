# Direct destruction and exile receipts

Drop of Honey and Porphyry Nodes first determine the least power among **all** creatures. Their direct destroy instruction then chooses among the destructible creatures with that power (CR 609.3). A separate instruction that chooses a creature, such as Imperial Edict, still permits choosing an indestructible creature. The normalizer preserves that distinction before desugaring direct destruction into a choice and an operation. Reference report: https://github.com/vishvananda/oracle-grammar/issues/12.

Frankenstein's Monster instructs its controller to exile X creature cards as it enters; this is an instruction, not an indivisible payment. The Source now performs as much as possible and compares the actual number exiled with X. The backend counts arrival object IDs whose recorded arrival zone is exile; redirects to other zones do not count and a later move does not erase a prior exile. Source report: https://github.com/i5jb/mtgish/issues/284; reference report: https://github.com/vishvananda/oracle-grammar/issues/13.

The retained worker scenario, corrected for the engine's graveyard order, fails against the old Drop generator because it offers an indestructible tied creature; it passes after the change. Controls cover only indestructible creatures, an indestructible least-power creature alongside a higher-power destructible one, and Imperial Edict independently choosing an indestructible creature. The Monster's retained partial-exile scenario fails old and passes new; exact-X and zero-X controls pass. The companion native integration probe verifies redirected exile and that an exile arrival ID retains its original zone after a later move. These proof inputs are in `rust_syntax/normalize/tests/fixtures/early_destroy_exile_native`; their original worker packets remain unchanged.

Wand of Ith's nonland branch previously repeated the land condition. Its Source now negates the second card predicate without treating an empty reveal as a nonland. The retained nonland-discard witness fails before and passes after; nonland-pay and land-pay-without-a-second-prompt controls pass. Reports: https://github.com/i5jb/mtgish/issues/285 and https://github.com/vishvananda/oracle-grammar/issues/15.
